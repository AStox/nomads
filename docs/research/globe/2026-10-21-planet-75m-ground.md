# The `ground` tile stage and the scatter (Unit 5): the island's soil, cover and objects on the whole 75 m tier

Owner: GroundStage. Code: `src/gpu/globe/ground.ts` (the stage, export `groundStage`), `ground.test.ts`; the island's kernels widened by a format (`src/gpu/wgsl/terrain-ground.ts` `GroundFormat`, `src/gpu/terrain/ground.ts` `groundWords`). R-numbers: R1860 (the reach of the nearest channel's power), R1861 and R1890 to R1899 for the scatter (below). Each test case alone: `lockf /tmp/nomads-gpu.lock bun test ./src/gpu/globe/ground.test.ts -t "<name>" --timeout 280000`.

## 1. The ground stage

### What was generalised, and how the island's goldens were kept
- `wgsl/terrain-ground.ts`: `groundWgsl(fmt)` with `GroundFormat {n, patch, reach}`. `ISLAND_GROUND` (n = 256, patch false) gives the island's text: the hash of the whole text (44,385 characters) is the same before and after (`de1c42fa0ed5a0fa`, `/tmp/planet-75m/ground-scratch/snap.ts`). A patch (`patch` true) is any side n; its `near` entry point (the nearest channel's power, two raster sweeps run one anti-diagonal at a time in one workgroup) takes every 256th column to a thread, and the sweep carries a channel's power no further than `reach` cells (`r <= reach` in `nearStep`).
- `terrain/ground.ts`: `groundWords(g, words, fmt)` is what `groundAndLeaf` ran, for either format; `pack` is exported.
- The island's goldens, one case at a time: ground on islands 1, 2, 3 (`8f1c7a4b7b625a7b`, `4d2b1ed687c1121a`, `de04b717fff3d82b`) pass. (The rearranged-rock case was run once, see the report.)
- Test of the widening on the island's own site (`ground.test.ts` "a patch of the island's size"): the patch format at n = 256 with a reach of 100,000 gives the island's words, all 27 outputs, hash for hash; with the stage's reach (64) no cell differs; with a reach of 8, 560 to 583 of island 1's 2,931 valley cells lose their alluvium, so the cap does act.

### Inputs (all per cell, read from the tier's files, mapped)
Heights `t4-height.i32`; open water = `t4-water.u16 > 0` or height <= 0 (the water stage's own rule); `t4-rock.u8`, `t4-drift.u8`, `t4-dd.u16` (terrain); `t4-chn.u8`, `t4-valley.u8`, `t4-pwr.u16` (x 4096 to Q16 W/m2), `t4-stb0..3.u16` and `t4-dry.u16` (1/256 m: the water stage stored them at 1/64 until this stage measured that 1/64 moves tree cover by more than 2/255 in 2,447 of 44,000 land cells of island 1 and by more than 20/255 in 475; at 1/256 999 and 10; no other input's quantisation moved a cover: heights, rain, evaporation, sun, temperature, humidity: 0 cells); `t4-pr/pe/sun/tmp/hum0..3` (air). `TABLE_UNIT` is the one constant that reads the tables' scale. A cube-corner patch cell (a repeat of the nearest cell) has no channel.

### Outputs (23 bytes a cell, 2.3 GB; table at the top of `ground.ts`)
`t4-tree.u8 t4-shr.u8 t4-grs.u8 t4-mar.u8 t4-bare.u8` (1/255 of the share; the beach's sand is none of the five), `t4-soil.u16` (1/1024 m), `t4-sflag.u8` (bit 0 silt, 1 sand, 2 peat), `t4-sandy.u8 t4-clayey.u8` (1/255), `t4-fert.u8`, `t4-moist.u8`, `t4-sw0..3.u8` (1/255), `t4-ph.u8` (1/30), `t4-org.u8` (organic %, 1/2.55), `t4-carb.u16` (t/ha, 1/16), `t4-nit.u16` (kg/ha/yr, 1/64). Not stored (the play area makes them again from the same inputs): the field layer's share and light, the leaf display. The pyramid's `tree shrub grass marsh bare` are the five covers, `soil` is `t4-soil.u16` /25.6 (u8 at 0.025 m); the scatter reads the covers, `sand`/`silt` flags, `clayey`, `soil`, `ph`, `moist`, `sw0..3`.

### The halo
A cell reads its four neighbours (the creep fluxes, the coast) and the nearest channel's power within `reach`. `GROUND_HALO` = 64 [design R1860: valley cells on islands 1, 2, 3 lie at most 24, 32 and 48 cells from a channel (measured, `reach.ts` in the scratch)]. A valley cell with no channel within 64 cells gets no alluvium [gap]. With the reach equal to the halo a cell 64 or more from the patch's edge depends on nothing outside it.

### Measured
- Tile independence (`ground.test.ts` "two patches half a tile apart"): patches of the same tile shifted by 128 cells, 32,768 shared cells, all 19 output files: **0 words differ**, tiles 458 (face 1, a 5.4 km range) and 223 (face 0 east edge). The sweep's tie order and the patches' orientation do not enter.
- One place from two faces ("one place seen from the two faces of a cube's edge"): 16,384 cells of face 0's east edge seen from a patch of face 0 and a patch of face 1 (axes flipped and turned, the cells 64 to 127 from the far patch's edge): **0 words differ**.
- Identity: the six tiles made twice (and resumed in two calls through `runTiles`) are the same bytes. Per tile (458, 1, 14, 1500, 937, 0): 0.08 to 0.11 s (mapped files hot in the page cache, the GPU shared): the planet is about 3 minutes of GPU and about as much of reading.
- Pinned (stand-in water, below): tile 458 `cd6f5602733af733`.

### Stand-in water
The water stage's files did not exist when this was written. The tests use a stand-in (`synthetic` in the test: channels where 20,000 cells drain, a floodplain within 24 cells on ground under 800 m, a table by the drainage area, standing water where the flood stands over the ground). The stage reads the water stage's files by their names and scales, which were confirmed by its author; the pin and the silt and peat counts are the stand-in's and must be re-recorded on the water stage's own files.

## 2. Gaps (not retuned)
1. The island's constants are Bornholm's: the species (pine, oak, ash, aspen, berry, hazel, heather), the leaf season's daylight ramp (the game's 55.1 N sky: 10 to 11 h; at the equator it keeps deciduous leaves off through the winter season of the year, which changes their carbon and, through the soil's organic matter, the second round's cover a little, not the compensation points that set the cover), the day's temperature range by season (Rønne's), the clay's pedotransfer and Clymo's bog. `similarityOf` has nothing to map: no constant here is a length along the surface set by a wind or a time of the atmosphere; the creep law is per metre of the real cell (CELL is 75 m, the tier's cells 63 to 75 m: gap shared with the water and air stages).
2. The ice's going (AGE = 18,500 years) is one value for every cell, as on the island.
3. A cube corner's patch repeats the nearest cell; the cells within one of a corner differ from what three faces' own would give (one ring).
4. The beach's sand (RUNUP 1.6 m above the sea) uses height 0 as still water; the tier has no tide or surge.
5. The stored covers are u8 (1/255): the scatter reads shares at 0.4%.
6. The mapped files are read by tile: `TileStage.inputs` is empty (`globalField` would hold 7 GB of them), `prepare` checks that the files exist.

## 3. The scatter (`scatter.ts`, `scatter.test.ts`; kernels `wgsl/terrain-scenery.ts`, host `terrain/scenery.ts`)

A pure function `scatterTile(ctx, tile, seed)` of the tile's stored fields, no object stored. A planet tile is 256 cells of 75 m, 19.2 km: the island's size, so the island's kernels are widened by `SceneryFormat` (`side`, `halo`, `shift`; `ISLAND_SCENERY` and `tileScenery(halo = 2)`): the cell reads go through `cellIx` (a field of the cells and a halo), the fine lattice's vertex u lies at cell coordinate (u - shift) / 4 (shift 0 on the island, whose lattice runs from the first cell's centre to the last's, 1,021 vertices; 2 on a tile, whose lattice runs from its own edge to its edge, 1,025 vertices and 1,024 quads a side, so that the lattices of neighbouring tiles meet and no quad is in two), and the game tile of a quad, the quad's neighbours' ranks, the mask texel of a vertex and the clear-quad ring follow from the shift. One text serves every tile: the tile's place (its corner in quarter metres on its face, the face) and the first quad's key are read from the buffers. The island's text is not identical (it is the same kernels with the format's constants), the island's output is: `scenery.test.ts` goldens for islands 1, 2, 3 (`eb2a09ded5988469`, `c3869bb03cf362ba`, `9959152ec0252928`), the clasts' counts island 1 and the integer-laws case, all unchanged after the change (the island's objects, fine field and mask, hash for hash).

- **Key (R1890)**: Philox (seed, layer, quad, object) with the quad's key = tile index (face * 256 + ty * 16 + tx) * 2^20 + the quad's number in the tile: 1,536 * 2^20 = 1.6e9 fits a word.
- **Halo (R1891)**: the fine field is a Catmull-Rom cubic read from one cell before to two after a vertex: a halo of 2 cells (`SCATTER_HALO`). Measured: a halo of 3 gives the same objects word for word. A tile's quads need nothing past it.
- **Noise (R1891)**: flora.ts's noise and fbm (the swell, the cover's wander, the patches of small plants) are read at the place's world position in the face's metres (`gx`, `gz`; the lattice wraps in 32 bits, the same for every tile that reads the place), so they run across a tile's edge; the face number is mixed into the hash (`faceKey`), so across a cube's edge, where the axes turn, the swell (2.6 m at 960 m) and the patches' density step [gap].
- **The stream mask (R1892, R1893)**: the island draws each river from `Hydro.rivers`' polylines (`shape`: chaikin twice, resample, meanders, joins), CPU list work over a whole river. A tile does not have the whole river, so what it draws is each channel cell's step to its receiver (`t4-to`, `t4-chn`, `t4-flow`): a segment from centre to centre stroked at the bankfull width (water.ts `bankWidth`, flow x BANKFULL, trees or shrubs over 5% of the ground) and the depth `bankDepth` gives, through the island's `raster` kernel, for the cells of the tile and the 8 round it (a river 1,200 m wide across would reach further). Chosen over calling `shape` (needs whole lines) and over a new GPU form (the cell chain needs none). Left out: the meanders and the smoothing: a polyline of 75 m steps [gap]. The mask and fine heights at the shared edge of two tiles are the same (below).
- **Output**: a `Scatter` in the island's form (`start` over the tile's 128 x 128 game tiles of 150 m, `kind`, `species`, `px`, `py` in game tiles from the tile's north-west corner, `size`, `seed`, `ore`); `fine` (heights, mask) on request.

### Measured (seed 1, key c418e5fe9b5e, the stand-in water: `scatter.test.ts`, each case alone)
- Made twice, and after another tile (458, 459, 458): equal; another seed: other objects, a count within 0.04%. A tile of halo 3 equals one of halo 2.

- The kinds follow the covers (tiles 458, 0, 14): trees a cell by tree cover (under 5, 5 to 30, 30 to 70, over 70%) 0.2 / 32 / 35 / 50 (tile 458); the count a cell is not monotone in the cover in tile 0 (1.4 / 71 / 59 / 50: stems fill their crowns' share, and poorer ground has smaller trees, so more of them), the bin with none holds a tenth of any other; tussocks 2 / 27 / 35 by grass cover; reeds 0.2 / 2.4 / 4.8 by marsh cover; every tree is pine, oak, ash or aspen and every bush a shrub of the island's; 0 of 682,000 trees (tile 458) and 39 of 1.56 million (tile 0) stand in a cell of standing water.
- Cost: tile 458 8.85 million objects (682,000 trees, 330 bog iron) in 0.15 s; **tile 0, a forested tile (25,000 of its 61,000 land cells over half trees): 6.9 million objects, 1.56 million trees, 0.17 s**; tile 14 5.2 million in 0.10 s; tile 1500 0.3 million in 0.11 s (the files hot, the GPU shared). The island (19.2 km, seed 1) holds 14.0 million entries by the same laws (the 3.3 million of the older count was before the low plants' layers): 1,536 tiles of the planet are of the same order, a few seconds a tile at the worst, never stored.

### Gaps (scatter)
1. The meanders and smoothing of the rivers (above); the mask is the cell chain.
2. Noise seam at a cube's edge (above).
3. The tile's objects read the u8 cover shares (0.4%) and the water stage's tables at the ground's own scale; the stand-in water above is not the water stage's: the stream counts (1,274 segments on tile 458) and the lakes are the stand-in's until its files are in.
4. The clasts' counts (boulders, stones, pebbles) are the island's per-cell Poisson draws and were not made for a tile (not asked: the sim's deadwood kinds read them).
5. Loose ore is capped at 65,536 a tile (`ORE_CAP`; the largest here 330); a marsh tile that passes it throws.
6. The species, densities (reeds 1.1 a quad on marsh, herbs, ferns, flowers, tussocks 20 a quad, bog iron 0.04) and sizes are the island's Bornholm values [gap, as the ground's].

## 4. The ground tiles (`groundtile.ts`, `bake.ts` GROUNDTILE_FIELDS, `scripts/globe-groundtiles.ts`)

For the page (Unit 6): one gzipped file a tier-4 tile, `groundtiles/f<face>/<ty>-<tx>.gt.gz` under the planet's bake directory, holding every field that scatter.ts and the close levels' painter (`demo/styles/isopixel/ground.wgsl.js packGround`) read, in the stage files' own words (only the receiver is coded). Layout: `[u32 LE header length][header JSON][planes in header.fields order, each 272 x 272 words, little-endian, row y by row, x along it]`. The field is the tile's 256 x 256 cells and a **halo of 8** all round it (the rivers' reach; the scatter reads 2 of it; cells past a face's edge are the face across's, laid flat as patch.ts does; the tile's own cells are x, y in [8, 264)). Header: `{version 1, seed, key, tile, face, tx, ty, halo 8, side 272, fields [{name, type, step, unit}] (the planes present), absent [names], lostReceivers, quick [4] | null}`; value = word x step. The table (names, types, steps, units, source file) is `GROUNDTILE_FIELDS`:

height i32 1/256 m; water u16 0.01 m; rock u8; moist u8; tree, shrub, grass, marsh, bare u8 1/255; flags u8 (bit 0 silt, 1 beach sand, 2 peat; the peat's depth is ground.ts PEAT_DEPTH); soil u16 1/1024 m; clayey u8; ph u8 1/30; winter u16 1/256 m (stb3); tab u16 1/256 m (the year's table); sun0..3 u16 1/1024; tmp0..3 i16 1/256 C; sw0..3 u8; pr1 u16 (the air stage's companded rain word of season 1: mm = (word / 360)^2); pe1 u16 0.1 mm; wsh u8 (wind share); fog u8; snp0..3 u16 0.25 mm of snow water; dir u8 (0 none, 1 to 8 the neighbour `DIRS[dir - 1]`); chn u8; flow u32 1/65536 m3/s; base u16 1/65536; under u16 1/4096 m3/s.

- **What the painter reads, and where each is.** `packGround`'s isle layers: height, water, windShare (`wsh`: AIR stores it), rock, precip and pet of season 1 (`pr1`, `pe1`), table (`tab`: WATER stores it), peat (flags bit 2), soil, silt (flags bit 0), fog (AIR), snow0 to snow3 (`snp0..3`: AIR); its `consts.quick` is the water stage's per-tile `quick` (header; null until WATER writes `quick.json`, an array of 1,536 four-number arrays). The fine layers (h, wet, moist, river mask, six covers) are not stored: the scatter's fine kernel makes them from these planes (`scatterTile(..., keepFine)`; `tileSource(gt)` gives scatter.ts the planes). The streams are the cell chain from `dir`, `chn`, `flow`, `base`, `under` (scatter.ts `riversOf(tileSource(gt), tile)`).
- **A layer no stage made** is left out of the file and named in `absent`, never filled: until the air stage stores `wsh`, `fog`, `snp0..3` and the water stage `tab`, `under`, `base` and `quick`, those are absent.
- **River lines, a gap**: the cell chain (75 m steps, joins at cells), no meanders or smoothing, because the island's `shape` needs a river's whole line.
- **Halo 8 for every plane**, one number for the page to remember (1.13 times the cells). Measured on tile 458 of the new planet (stand-in water, planes: 40): 1,546,396 bytes gzipped, 20.9 a cell, **2.38 GB for 1,536 tiles**.
- **Tests (`groundtile.test.ts`)**: a tile read back is the stage files' words in every plane (39 planes over 73,984 cells), the receivers decode to the stage's, the scatter of the unpacked tile equals the scatter of the stage files (tile 458: 1,601,672 objects, same hash, same segments), a call stops at its budget and the next resumes.
- **Rain's word moved**: the air stage companded its rain (`precipWord`); `ground.ts` now reads it that way (`rainQ16`), integer arithmetic.

## 1. The global discharge (R1835)
`dischargeOf(gpu, dir, tier, fields)` sorts the whole forest of `t4-to.u32` by depth (pointer jumping, `Regional.forest`: 1.1 to 1.3 s, deepest path 2,339 cells, no flood, no tier table) and sums a u32 weight file down it exactly in 64 bits (`area64`: low word in ACC, high in ER). Output per weight `stem`: `<stem>-lo.u32` + `<stem>-hi.u16` (bits 32 to 47; a weight under 2^21 a cell cannot pass 2^48), a count of cells (`ONES`) is `<stem>.u32`. The water uses the four seasons' surplus (`t4-q0..3`, 1/64 m3 a season: the water kernel's own Q6 volume) and `t4-cells`; the year is their sum. 2.8 GB on disk (6 B x 4 + 4 B a cell).
- Staged forest crossing a face edge (tier 1, weights about 4e8 a cell): 0 of 1.5 M cells wrong against the CPU twin, 10,000+ cells past 2^32, largest sum above 2^40; `forest(to)` alone gives the same bytes as the flood's own tree.
- Real tier 4 (key c418e5fe9b5e), 1,000 cells walked up the forest on the CPU (the largest basins' mouths, other mouths, hillslope and river cells): 0 wrong, 41 past 32 bits, largest basin 1,665,951 cells. Cost: sort 1.1 s, each field 1.4 to 2.1 s including its write; the 5 fields of the water 9 s in all (load of the machine: other agents running; upper bound).

## 2. The water stage
Per tile on a patch (side 256 + 2 x 96 = 448) in the planet's format `planetWater(side)` (a window's format: Q15.17 m heads, Q16.16 m3/s flows, inflow words), the island's own kernels, generalised to any side by a format argument. How the island path was kept: the new text is generated from the same source, `cx(i)`/`cy(i)` are `& 255`/`>> 8` for a power-of-two side, the aquifer's per-cell step is one function `aq_cell` called by the island's anti-diagonal kernel and by the planet's red-black kernel; the planet-only words (void cells, lakes at the flooded surface, no flow through the ring, 1.9 relaxation, 0.5 mm tolerance) are generated only under `fmt.planet`. Checked: `water.test.ts` seeds 1, 2, 3 (goldens 1456e5487361e378, b82e86c570fc740c, 116cf847c3c043b0) and `window.test.ts` "a staged window is the same bytes twice" (39e4d2f8d7203637) and "a window's channels drain" (77.2%, 98.6%, 379, 1.0%) pass, run one case at a time in a worktree of HEAD carrying my two kernel files (the main tree's island goldens currently move with the air agent's unfinished rain/climate edits, not with water). 16-bit sort keys: the island's `levels`/`gather` already use 14 jumping rounds and u32 indices (depth up to 8,190, flagged beyond).
- Receivers: the planet's `t4-to.u32` mapped with `localOf` (outside the patch: an outlet, `to = self`); flooded surface `t4-flooded.i32`; sea = height <= 0.
- Hollows (R1830): `t4-basin.u8`, made once over the whole tier by `basinOf` (CPU, 4-neighbour components of cells the flood raised by 1 cm, across tile and face edges; a component whose deepest point is over 3 m is a lake): 21.4 M land cells; a lake stands at the flooded surface at every cell, every lake spills (the island's balance for a part-full lake needs the whole hollow and its flows). The island's `hollows`/`lakecand` (O(B^2)) are not run in the planet format.
- Inflow (R1833): for each cell outside the patch whose receiver is inside, its global discharge (cells and four seasons' volumes) is added at the receiver as exact 64-bit words (`Inflow.q6`). Identity tested: over the patch's own forest each cell's surplus plus the inflow gathers to exactly the global discharge at every cell of the patch (4 seasons); and with no rain in a lake-free patch the kernel's flow in every cell away from the sea and lakes is the CPU's gather of the inflow words, to the word (0 of the cells wrong).
- Void cells (R1834): a patch at a cube's corner repeats the nearest cell where a corner leaves none: such a cell is a sea cell with zero transmissivity.
- Aquifer (R1832): a planet's patch holds no head at its ring (no water crosses its outer ring of cells; the island's ring held at the ground less 3 m fed water into the ground), is relaxed in two colours across all the grid's workgroups (`aqrb`, CHUNK 64 sweeps a submit, the change of the last sweep read back) at 1.9 to a head change under 0.5 mm. The island's 1.6 and 2 mm stop 0.7 m short of the answer on a grid this size (the stopping change over 1 - rho); the sums of head and flow agreed to 1e-6 between 0.5 and 0.25 mm. Over-relaxation above 1.93 stalls on the integer arithmetic's noise (changes of 100 to 130 units). Caps 80,000 sweeps a solve; a tile that needs more throws.
- Climate: `ClimateSource` per cell, never a window's value. Until the air stage's files exist: `planetRain(planet)` (the planet's tier-0 rain per season sampled to the cell; potential evaporation = rain less runoff) [gap: not a PET]. `climateFiles(pr, pet, perMm)` reads u16 files when they exist (names to wire from the air notes).
- Drift/rock/thickness: the `terrain` stage's files; the thickness is held where rock + drift transmissivity would pass the sweep's u32 word (a format's bound).
- Outputs (documented at the top of water.ts): `t4-water.u16` (cm), `t4-flow.u32` (Q16 m3/s), `t4-qwet.u32` (wettest season, Q16), `t4-base.u16` (Q16), `t4-chn.u8`, `t4-valley.u8`, `t4-pwr.u16` (1/16 W/m2), `t4-stb0..3.u16` and `t4-dry.u16` (table depth, 1/64 m): 2.6 GB. Not kept: springs, `under`, the year's table, slope (from the heights).
- Reading the 403 MB global files: mapped (`Bun.mmap`), a tile touches only its patch's pages; `TileStage.inputs` is empty because `globalField` would hold 10 GB of them resident (suggest the same in stages.ts).

## 4. Gaps (not retuned)
1. **Lakes leak.** A lake holds the ground's head fixed at its level, as the island's; in dry mountains with a high-transmissivity rock it is a source with no inflow behind it (the gather then clamps its negative net at 0, so the leaked water appears in rivers beside it). Flow over the global discharge at cells over 0.3 m3/s: tile 14 (coast) 0.93, tile 0 1.0, tile 1 2.8, 937 5.8, 1500 26, 458 20 (median ratios). Sealing the lake bed (transmissivity 0 or 1/64) left 458 at 12 or made it fail to settle: the ground then has no anchoring head. The proper fix is the island's own balance made global: a hollow holds a lake only if its inflow (the global discharge, exact) exceeds its evaporation (`lake_mm` over the spill depth), decided once over the tier, with leakage limited to its inflow.
2. **Inflow is the surplus, not the island's weights** (surplus less recharge plus ground water out less lake evaporation): upstream cells outside the patch lack those corrections. Measured as the flow difference between halos (above), 1 to 23 of 65,536 m3/s here; a two-pass (weights out, gather again, rerun) would close it.
3. **The aquifer has long modes.** Where no drain, lake or sea anchors the head over a patch (about 2 of 24 tiles, 29,824 to 42,688 sweeps), the change falls 4x per 12,800 sweeps; the stop (0.5 mm a sweep) leaves a metres-deep error in the table that depends on the patch: tile 854 (face 3): two patches differ by 3.4 m anywhere and 0.9 m within 5 m of the ground. Not tile independent there. A coarse-grid correction or a global aquifer solve is the fix.
4. Dry hollows: no lake is dropped, so flow passes through every hollow (the island cuts at a closed lake).
5. Stand-in climate (see above) until the air stage's files; the planet's rain (212 mm a year on land) leaves most tiles with surplus near zero.
6. `basinOf` runs on the CPU once (10 s class), its output is a function of the flood only.
7. The hydrology's constants are per 75 m cell (`CELL`); tier 4's cells are 63 to 75 m (grid gap 7); no radius-dependent constant is read, so `similarityOf` has nothing to map here.
8. Tests that cannot be run here: second GPU adapter; the full 1,536-tile run (30 to 45 min of GPU) was not made.

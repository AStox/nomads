## What is wrong, then, and the three changes

1. **The shoreline is where the fine field crosses the sea, and the hold must not know the coast.** The band hold fits the land's loss only on land cells (`btally_*`, `bgw`, `raise`), so a coastal tier-0 cell's submerged children never rise with the land and the carry's mask stays. Uplift and rebound are crustal: they lift every column of a tier-0 cell, covered or not (Whipple and Tucker 1999: U is rock uplift against base level), and what the rivers shed is deposited on the submerged part of the same cell. Build: the Gram system and the raise run over all cells of a tier-0 cell that has any land (a mask-free hold); a submerged child that reaches the sea level becomes land at one unit; the sea level stays a threshold, never a mask. Test: land share, tier-0 box error, coast positions against the old and A2 coasts; kill if the shelf under 100 m of whole cells floods, or the tier-0 means (meanErr p99) rise.
2. **A coast is shaped by the sea.** On a low coast (the river and K3 plains, and the backs of spits) the sea level crossing of a 1 m plain is set by what the waves and rivers do within a metre, which no fluvial law resolves. The cited law is the cell model of Ashton, Murray and Arnoult 2001 (Nature 414: 296 to 300) and Ashton and Murray 2006 (J. Geophys. Res. 111: F04011): alongshore flux Q = K cos^(6/5)(phi) sin(phi) of the angle between the waves and the shoreline, instability above 42 degrees, diffusion below, sand conserved. It is cheap (shoreline cells only, 1024 by 1024 at 75 m is some thousands). Wave climate: the planet's wind (`seawind.ts` zonal mean). Applies only where a coast cell is backed by land under the active profile's height (a rock coast is not a sand coast). Prototype on CPU on the tier-5 region states first; pictures decide whether it goes into the chain. Kill if the capes are the same shape everywhere (one climate) or move sand across tier-0 means by more than 1 m.
3. **Fine relief where relief can exist.** Keep the hold's lift as the uplift (no second law: a prescribed U on top of it breaks the tier-0 mean; the bound above). Fix what is left of lowland flatness in the carry and the sea: the lake of K3 and the 1-unit puddles are dry hollows of tier 0 and of the integer ramp, not lakes in the baked world (water stage, 3 m rule): judge them as the water stage sees them. The harness is extended to cube edges and tx 0 (E2) so all nine places are drawn.

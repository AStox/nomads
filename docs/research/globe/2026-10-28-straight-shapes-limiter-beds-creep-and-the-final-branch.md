## Where the relief is (and is not) lost, and rock-and-climate creep: not adopted

- Relief is not lost between tier 0 and the regions: the four regions are lowland. Land p99 / max of the same place, tier 0 to tier 5: R1 1600 / 1786 to 1574 / 2070 m, home 2535 / 2944 to 2575 / 3411, the four highest tier-3 windows (M1 to M4) 4602 / 4452 / 4449 / 3844 m at tier 0 to 4925 / 4561 / 4517 / 4022 m at tier 5 (max 5.3 km). Tiers 4 and 5 add relief (p99 0 to +320 m, max +100 to +660 m). The planet is gentler than the old one because tier 0 is: land mean 524 against 1351 m, p99 3323 against 4620 m, share of land above 1000 m of 10 km relief .051 against .179 (the relief window made tier 0 Earth-like by Montgomery and Brandon: under .05).
- Slope at fixed relief class matches the old planet in the mountains within 3 to 14% (500 to 1000 m class 15.9 against 15.5 degrees; 1000 to 1500 m 20.4 against 23.7); the regions are 25 to 45% below it in the lowland classes. Of the regions' mean-slope gap to the old planet (5.3 against 13.8 degrees), 68 to 91% is the relief distribution and 9 to 32% slope at fixed class. At equal own relief both planets sit at about .23 / .43 / .63 / .72 / .77 of the cited S(Rz) (relief classes 250 to 2000+ m): the old planet misses the 0.8 to 1.25 gate too; slides cap slope at 30 to 36 degrees, so S above about 2300 m of relief is unreachable.
- The carry only interpolates (matched 500 to 1000 m class: 4.5 degrees at 2.4 km, 6.1 at 300 m); tier 4 does all the dissection (ridge to valley relief in 1 km windows 72 to 169 m in the mountains, 49 to 89 m in the regions; the old planet is at 235 m at tier 3 from its eroded tiers 1 to 3); tier 5 adds almost nothing. The fine tiers are under-run, not exhausted: response time 370 to 559 My against 266 My of schedule; 96 My more raise the matched 150 m slope 10 to 25% (inference: asymptote near the old planet's); the band hold costs 10 to 15% of fine-scale slope against the earlier smooth-uplift law at R1.
- Rock-and-climate creep (Hurst et al. 2013 Table 1 fit, no beds) does not help: slope falls 8 to 29% in the four regions (slope over S(Rz) .29 to .43 down to .23 to .38), river straight30 rises 2.5 to 4.7 times, F4 gets MORE regular (CV .216 to .099, ratio 31 to 72). The slide threshold per rock already exists and barely binds (slope over .5 on .06 to 2% of land); K x100 (the ratio of tier 0's relief-law rate, 256 to 699 m/My, to the fine tiers' loss, 2.5 to 3.6 m/My) and real rain lower slope; running R1 tier 4 to 3 response times saturates at slope over S(Rz) .43. Inference, not built: the fine tiers have no uplift (each step's loss is lifted back), so relief decays toward an attractor; a law giving the fine tier the tier-0 relief law's rate as its uplift, with tier-0 heights held at the end, is the untested candidate.

## Decision

Bake `globe-v13r-v14r-final` as is (the limiter in, beds and creep out). Open gaps, reported and not tuned: F4's regular spacing, lattice-locked coasts, gentle lowlands and slope over S(R) at .22 to .77 (the old planet's own value is similar at equal relief), land share .24 to .25 against .29 and mean 528 m against 673 to 840 m, the stepped parallelogram coast (start state), no uplift law for the fine tiers.

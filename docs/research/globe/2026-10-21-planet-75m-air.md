# The `air` tile stage (Unit 5): the island's rain and climate on the whole 75 m tier

Owner: AirStage. Code: `src/gpu/globe/air.ts` (the stage and its host), `air.test.ts`; widened by a format argument, the island's format giving the text it always had (checked against a snapshot of every kernel's source and of the parameter words, and by the stage tests' goldens): `src/gpu/terrain/{rain,climate}.ts`, `src/gpu/wgsl/terrain-{rain,climate}.ts` (and `src/terrain/climate.ts` exports `codeRose`). R-numbers R1800 to R1829 (the rain and climate): R1800 the patch format of the rain (coarse 4, per-cell background and turn, wide dispatch), R1801 the block's rain kept (out = shape made / mean made, then P0 / mean), R1802 the cloud delays by similarity (s), R1803 the per-cell place (sea-level air, sea, air's water), R1804 the sun's band of latitude and the grid's axes, R1805 the wind's direction per cell and the rose in the patch's sense, R1806 the snow years' groups, R1807 the shore's seed, R1808 the wider words (blur sum, the shore in Q12, the wake's distance saturating, the snow years' shift 4), R1809 the inverse's 1 / M^2 for any M.

## What was generalised, and how the island's goldens were kept

- `wgsl/terrain-rain.ts`: `RainFormat {n, coarse, cells, wide}` (`ISLAND_RAIN` = the island's): the grid's side, the block (4 cells, the island's), per-cell inputs (`cells`: each cell's background rain and the turn of the wet rose, read between two sectors of the rose as `turned` does), a two-row dispatch for more than 65,535 groups (`wide`), the twiddle index by `%` where M is not a power of two, and a workgroup of 256 taking every 256th frequency where M is not 256. `terrain/rain.ts`: `rainParams(…, fmt)`, `rainWords`, `cloudDelays(ice, s)` (the atmosphere's rounding of Earth's times s), and the 1 / M^2 of the inverse (the kernels' shifts hold it for M = 256 only: the first widened run was a factor of 4 too wet at M = 512).
- `wgsl/terrain-climate.ts`: `ClimateFormat {n, patch}` and `layoutOf` (the arena's sections and the table buffer's words by format); every kernel's `256` and `255` is the side; with `patch`: per-cell sea-level air, sea and air's water by season (sections `tS`, `seaS`, `e0S`), the wind's turn (`wturn`, the rose turned per cell in `air_reduce`), the band of latitude (`band`: the sun's rows of that band, in the compass's axes, the normal turned to them by the grid's axes `cps sps` and the horizon's bearing offset by `psi`), the snow years over host-made groups of four cells, the schedule's list holding `bearing * cells + cell`, and the wider words below. `terrain/climate.ts`: `PatchInput`, `runClimate` for either format, `schedule` per side.
- Kept byte for byte: the WGSL text of every island kernel (hash snapshot `/tmp/planet-75m/air-scratch/snap.ts`, taken before the change), the rain's parameter words, and the goldens (below).

Island goldens run one case at a time after the last change (`goldens.log` in the scratch directory; results in the final report): rain.test.ts 9 cases, climate.test.ts 5 cases; `upstream`, `water`, `ground`, `scenery`, `island` and `world` tests read neither file's changed paths and were not run.

Rain is stored as `round(sqrt(mm) * 360)`: u16, 0.006 mm held at a millimetre, 0.35 mm at 32,000 (`precipOfWord`); the other fields linear. 47 bytes a cell: 4.73 GB for 100.7 million cells. The consumers read them back by the unit (`/ 10`, `/ 1024`, `/ 256`, `/ 255`, the snowpack `/ 4`; the rain by `(word / 360)^2`). Not stored, in `AirTile` (the play area is one tile and is made again in about a second): the snow's share of precipitation, sky, pool, breeze, salt and the lee by bearing. Reading the whole files per stage (`globalField`) is 200 MB a u16 field: a stage that reads all the seasonal fields would hold several GB, so the downstream stages should read the tile's rows (`FieldFile.readRow`) not whole files.

## The halo, and what it cuts

Reach in cells of 75 m: the blur 24, the normal 1, the pool 25, the horizons 95 (the largest that is exact), the snow years' group 2; the wind's wake (30 of a break's heights: kilometres behind a range), the salt's far fall-off (414 cells) and the rain's lee waves (the 17 km mountain wave of the theory, damped here by delays that are s times Earth's) are past any halo. AIR_HALO = 128 [design: a multiple of 16 (a block of tier 0) over the horizon's 96]. A patch is 512 by 512: four times the tile's cells.

Measured on tile 458 (face 1, a 5.4 km range with land and sea), a patch of halo 128 against one of halo 192 (`/tmp/planet-75m/air-scratch/indep.ts`), root mean square of the difference in the cells 128 to 160 from the 128-patch's edge (the tile's own edge cells), the worst cell in brackets, against the field's 99th percentile:

Over the whole patch (0 to 192 from the edge) the rain's rms does not fall with distance (107, 78, 79, 37, 37, 39, 29, 21, 19 mm in the bins 0-8, 8-16, 16-32, 32-48, 48-64, 64-96, 96-128, 128-160, 160-192): the rain is not local, because the transfer's lee waves travel. It is a gap: **the rain's orographic part is a function of the relief far beyond any halo; at 128 its rms against a halo of 192 is 1% of the field's p99, the worst cell (one windward face taking a block's rain) a quarter of it.** Two patches of one tile that overlap by 128 cells, the shared interior (each 128 or more from both edges; `air.test.ts`): precipitation rms 22 mm of p99 1,008 (spring), 4.0 of 247 (winter); evaporation 0.33 of 419; snow cover 0.0045 (worst 0.09); salt 0.001 (worst 0.011); the sun, temperature, humidity, breeze, sky and pool 0 (the same words). On the staged planet (hills of 150 m everywhere) the rain's rms is 8% of its mean, worst 1.6x: the staged hills are an extreme case.

The wake's reach, shown by the same table at halo 64: breeze rms 0.05 to 0.13 m/s out to 96 cells, the wind share 0.03: a cell's answer under a break whose shelter passes the halo is a function of what stands further out.

## The seam from latitude (sun table)

A patch's one latitude would step the year's flat-ground sun between two adjacent tiles (5.62 degrees of latitude on this planet) by 0.6% (0 N), -1.95 (15), -4.6 (30), -7.7 (45), -10.1 (60), -8.9 (75), -4.7% (85 N): `seam.ts`. The sun's band of each cell (0.1 degree) makes it 0.02 to 0.19% a band, the same word whichever patch holds the cell: sun and temperature are equal word for word across overlapping patches. The rain's C_w and delays still take the patch centre's sea-level air by season: two adjacent tiles' centres differ by up to the planet's gradient of the sea-level air (some 0.75 K a tile at the equator-to-pole contrast, 4% in C_w): the step in the orographic part of the rain is that fraction of a few percent: not separately measured (it is inside the overlap's rain rms above, whose two patches have centres 128 cells apart).

## Cost, measured

Per tile on the real planet (seed 1, `c418e5fe9b5e`, a tile of 256 by 256 cells in a patch of 512 by 512), `runTiles` over tiles 458, 1 and 14: 1.40, 1.11 and 1.01 s (the first reads the two 400 MB inputs); the stage's own parts on tile 458: gather 0.1 s, geometry 0.04, the planet's fields at the patch (sampleCoarse, 28 fields) 0.2, the GPU run and readback 0.66. About 1.1 s a tile: 1,536 tiles take about 28 minutes in seven calls of 270 s. Taken with the machine at load average 4 to 4.5, swap nearly full (8.7 of 10 GB), and the GPU shared with another agent's stage runs: an upper bound. Memory of the process: the two input files 0.8 GB, a patch's arena 190 MB and its tables 22 MB on the GPU. A halo of 96 (448 by 448) would cost 0.77 of that; of 192 (640 by 640) 1.6.

## Gaps (open)

1. **The rain is smooth, its block means are not exact** (R1811, replaces the block-constant rain: the planet's rain was a constant to a block in the kernel's background and in the last scale, so the rain stepped at every block edge; R1810's cap now acts on the enhancement cell by cell). The background is the planet's rain read by `sampleCoarse` (bilinear between the four tier-0 centres, as the air and the wind are), in the kernel and as the factor; the enhancement is made / (the blocks' means of made, read between their centres), held at `RAIN_CAP` = 4 [design: Bornholm's gauges rise 17% of the island's rain for each 100 m (R1327), 2.7 times over a kilometre, the theory's gain over them 3.5 (the rain note, finding 5)]; each block's mean is then brought to its tier-0 cell's rain by a scale that is read between the blocks' centres, `SCALE_PASSES` = 8 corrections of it, and no cell passes 32,000 mm [derived: the words downstream hold Q16 in an i32: 32,767 mm]. Measured on tile 458 (`air.test.ts`): of 130 blocks of 50 mm or more a season the error against the tier-0 rain has median 9e-4, p90 3.5e-3, worst 6%; summed over all blocks 1.9% of the rain, the tile's total 1.018 of the planet's. The remaining error is not rounding: the planet's bilinear rain does not keep a block's mean where the tier-0 rain changes fast (a block beside a much drier one gets some of its neighbour's rain from the interpolation, and a smooth scale cannot take it back out: a block of tier-0 rain 0 beside wet ones holds 20 mm) [gap]. Edge-step ratio (the mean |d rain| across a block's edge over the mean inside blocks; 1 is no step): tile 458 0.87 (spring), 0.71 (winter) (summer and autumn are dry there: no variation, counted 1); the staged ridge 0.47 to 0.54 (the ridge's waves put more change inside blocks than across their edges). Before: a constant to each block, a step at every edge. **Not tile independent beyond its first drift** (above): rms 1% of p99, worst cell a quarter of it; the lee waves travel. The snow years' shift is 6 [derived: 4 x 32,000 mm a season is 128,000 mm a year, under 3,000 x 2^6], the snow years' mean of four cells' rain is taken at 13 bits so that four cells of 32,000 mm sum inside a word; the transforms' heights are scaled by 16 [derived: a patch of mean detail under 1.9 km a cell], doubling where a patch passes it (none does). The rain is stored companded (above).
2. **The wake and the salt's far fall-off** reach past the halo: breeze rms 0.013 (worst 0.5 m/s) and wind share 0.0017 (worst 0.09) in the tile's edge cells at halo 128; the salt is seeded at the edge by the planet's distance to the sea (a floor of the air's distance over land along the wind): an inland tile's salt is 0.041 on average where unseeded it is 0.070, the seeded 128 against 192 rms 0.0027 (unseeded 0.0051).
3. **The grid is read as a square one of 75 m cells**, as the island's kernels read theirs: the cube's cells are 66 to 75 m wide and their axes are 0 to 30 degrees from a right angle (median 0.1 degree at a face's centre, 27 at the corner tiles, 10 at (3, 12) of face 1). A skew of 27 degrees reads a slope 0.7 to 1.1 times its size by direction. Across a face's edge the same cells seen from the two faces' patches agree: sky exact, temperature 0.02 C (worst 0.13), the sun in the low-sun season 8% rms, 0.5% in the other (`air.test.ts` "one place seen from the two faces"). The remedy is the metric's terms in the normal, the horizon's steps and the wavefront's bearings, or a patch resampled to the tangent plane; neither was built.
4. **The Taylor and Lee speed-up is not capped**: on 5 km relief the 600 m "around" gives breezes to 9 m/s against the coast's 4.5 (the quarter of the planet's land on a range: p99.9 8.3 m/s), which raises Penman's aerodynamic term and lowers the sun's warming (`dI / (32.7 + 42.2 u)`); before the words were widened (the blur's sum and the shore's distance overflowed) it was 55 m/s and 256 of salt.
5. **The planet's sea-level wind speed is 0.07 m/s** (Unit 4's gap) and the rain's wet-hours' wind, the sea wind and the roses are Bornholm's: the rain and the breeze take the planet's direction only.
6. **The humidity is stored in 1/255** (the ground reads Q16), and **precipitation and evaporation at 0.1 mm**; the sun at 1/1024 MJ/m2 a day; temperature 1/256 C.
7. **Cloud, humidity at sea level, fog and beam/glow are Bornholm's** at every latitude (no tropical cloud, no polar night's glow beyond the sun's rows).
8. **The planet's seasons are the atmosphere's four** and the sun's table takes Bornholm's cloud through them: a place in the southern hemisphere has the planet's "summer" at the sun's northern summer; the stage does not turn them.
9. **Pinned hashes are this engine's, this planet's** (key `c418e5fe9b5e`, the heights as baked 2026-10-21 after the uplift fix): tile 458's stored words `9a283abb8e7c719` (smooth rain, below). Re-pin when the bake or the atmosphere changes.
10. **The planet's own rain is wetter than the words**: seed 1's tier-0 cells hold up to 19,562 mm in a season and 50,276 mm a year (seeds 2 and 3: 21,605 and 23,090 a season, 52,457 and 52,576 a year), which the first version's snow years' words (48,000 mm a year) and a linear 0.1 mm store (6,553) could not hold. The planet's tropical rain is Unit 4b's gap; here it is carried, not changed.

# climate.ts: what each constant should be, Bornholm reference (GenClimate)

I did not edit any repository file. My scripts and data are in /tmp/nm/gen/: `born.py`, `born2.py`, the DMI JSON pulls, and `born.json` / `born2.json`.

**Two things to know before the table:**
- **Game seasons.** In sky.ts each season is centred on an equinox or solstice. Spring therefore runs 4 Feb to 5 May, not March to May. I averaged every season below over those windows: for DMI monthly values I interpolated daily or spread each month's total evenly over its days; for ISD hourly data I binned the hours directly.
  - air.ts sec. 33 (CHAIN, SPEED, RANGE) uses March-to-May style seasons, about 6 weeks off. That is out of my scope, but you may want to check it.
- **Sources** (all fetched this session):
  - **DMI** = DMI open climate API, https://opendataapi.dmi.dk/v2/climateData. I used station 06190 Bornholms Lufthavn (Rønne), station 06193 Hammer Odde, municipality 0400 Bornholm and the 10 km grid, monthly values 1991–2020. Some parameters only go back to 2011.
  - **ISD** = NOAA ISD hourly, https://noaa-global-hourly-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/{yr}/{id}.csv, 2015–2024. Stations: Hammer Odde 06193099999 (SYNOP, which has total cloud), Rønne 06190099999 (METAR, whose cloud groups are not total cover, so I did not use them for cloud) and Visby 02590099999.
  - **Tejn** = DMI ocean API, station 32048 Tejn Havn, water temperature `tw`, 2005–2024: https://opendataapi.dmi.dk/v2/oceanObs.

## Structural flags

1. **SEASONS columns.** `cloud` becomes two fields, beam and diffuse transmittance. Add `humid0`. `wet` stays. Add a background rain rate `P_bg` (625 mm/yr). Add per-season wet-hour wind, wet share and wet rose for the Smith & Barstad runs.
2. **Removed:** `wetness` and the rescale, `marine` (no consumers), `lifted`, and every per-cell air fraction. `fog` changes meaning to the cloud-base climatology.
3. **Wind rose:** the parametric `rose` becomes a measured 16-sector table, rotated by `prevailing`.
4. **Snow:** with the Bornholm table, the smooth daily harmonic never reaches 1.3 °C at sea level (Rønne has 5–7 % of winter and spring days at or below 0 °C). Snow at sea level then comes only from the s_eff spread. Real Bornholm gets more snow than that implies, which a day-to-day weather term would add.
5. **Smith & Barstad** needs an FFT over the grid and an upper-air wind; the generator has neither. That wind is a [gap].
6. **Downstream:** air.ts POOL_C, CHAIN and SPEED are calibrated to Belmullet and to March–May style seasons. They should be re-derived for Bornholm on the solar-centred windows, by the same method as sec. 33.
7. **What to check after the change:** generator outputs (snow cover, PET, lakes) and niche fits, since temperatures move from 24/−4 °C to 14.7/3.7 °C. Run the gate or worlds probes.

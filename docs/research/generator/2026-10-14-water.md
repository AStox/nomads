Bornholm facts used: net precipitation 270–350 mm/yr, ~90 % of it leaving by streams. Subsurface outflow 16–110 mm/yr. Mean runoff 249 mm (7.9 l/s/km²); median minimum 0.1 l/s/km², with "many streams dry in summer". Median maximum ~140 l/s/km² over the granite north. 416 km of designated streams (986 km counting ditches) on 588 km²; 18 lakes, 93 ha. The water table lies "a few metres" below terrain in basement (BA06, OV00, GEUS10).

## Structural changes flagged
1. Hydro gains `channel` (a·S² mask), plus per-cell `qbf` and slope `S`. River tuples become `[x, y, mean, base, Qbf, S]`. `QMIN`/`TRICKLE` exports go (land.ts, flora.ts, streams.ts, inspect.ts).
2. `widthOf`/`depthOf` take Q_bf, bank tree-cover share (looked up at use time; ground.ts runs after hydrology), D50 and S. Current width and depth follow at-a-station from Q/Q_bf.
3. ROCKS gets K (m/s) and thickness b in place of `perm` (with GenRockErosion). The fault-valley fill needs its own T.
4. Seasonal groundwater storage (Robbedale sand Sy 0.04; fault-valley wells S 4.5e-6–3.5e-3, GEUS10). Without it, baseflow cannot recede and rejected recharge cannot return in the same season; today `gw/S` spreads seepage evenly over the year.
5. Per-cell TAW needs soil before `hydrology()`, but ground() reads `table`/`valley`: iterate, or split ground into pre- and post-hydrology passes.
6. Delta removed. The floodplain needs a T-year flood ratio (OV00 Bilag 8, not extracted).

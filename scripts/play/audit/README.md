# Audit gates (2026-10-24)

The three auditors' scripts (perf, far, close; their findings were in /tmp/globe-audit, summarised in docs/plans/2026-10-20-001-feat-seamless-globe-plan.md Unit 9) and the lead's own, kept here
because /tmp is not. They are scratch scripts, not suites: each launches headless Chrome with its own profile, so every one is run as `lockf /tmp/nomads-gpu.lock bun <script>` and closes its browser in
`finally`. Paths inside them (`/tmp/globe-fix-lead2/chrome`, `/tmp/globe-fix-lead2/out`, the site port) are the lead's: change them or keep that directory.

- `mksite.sh <worktree> <outdir>`: builds the styles demo and injects the perf instrument (`perfprelude.js` beside it, wrapping GPUDevice, queue and buffers) as the render worker's first import. `perf/server.ts <port> <dir>` serves such a site (no-store, request log).
- perf/lib.ts is the harness (launch, load, take, summarize, drag, settleTo). Gates by item: `dive.ts` (load, wheel out to orbit and back: ready, errors, rates; `SITE=` picks the site), `idle.ts` and
  `spikes.ts` (frame skipping; a long pan's GPU frames), `cuts.ts` (largest ladder grid and views cut, per place), `dcost.ts` (D's cost), `det.ts`, `still.ts`, `cmp.ts`, `open.ts` (same picture
  between loads and builds; scripts/play/loadsame.ts is the gate), `bloss.ts`, `badge.ts`, `soak.ts`, `arrive.ts`, `band.ts`, `profpan.ts`, `loadtest.ts`, `netdive.ts` (the auditor's), `lakes.ts`
  (old against new site stills at a lake), `edge.ts`, `spritejump.ts`, `probe*.ts`; `cmpq.ts <siteA> <siteB>` (stills of two builds at D, A, B, with `QUERY=`, `PLACES=`; `?pg=0` puts the ladder's own ground in them), `inout.ts` (3.1: the same view reached zooming in and out).
- far/lostcheck.ts: 30 wheel ticks out from the opening view, losses in N runs. close/t3.ts, t3b.ts: drift of sprites against ground after a pan; t10.ts: window builds after a jump.
- `staircase.ts <lat> <lon> [--bake dir] [--seam]`: CPU only; the share of a window's stream samples that turn 30 degrees or more within 19 m, before (the cell chain) and after the smoothing (`src/gpu/globe/streamcurve.ts`), how far the nodes move, and with `--seam` that the neighbouring tile's window draws the same curve over the shared cells.
- Drags (real mouse, button held; `PROFILE=` and `SITE=` pick the browser's directory and the site; lib.ts reads PROFILE when a script names none): `grab.ts` (ten cases at cube edges, corners, a window swap and a pole: the grabbed ground against the cursor), `lurch.ts` (a steady drag's target step against the median), `orbitdrag.ts` (orbit zoom, from latitudes 42 to 89.96, steps of 3 to 20 px, press points from the centre to past the planet's edge: the grabbed ground's and the old target's screen step against the mouse's).
- Stalls after a teleport: `perf/stall.ts --zooms=1.7,3,4.2,5 [--places=P1,P2,A,B,R,E2] [--secs=5] [--prof]` sets `play.view.geo` to each place at each zoom (the page's own opening, a settled zoom between) and records the page's largest rAF gap, the worker's largest frameDone gap and the picture's delay (how long after the first frame the page sent since the last picture was drawn the next picture came: a gap with no frame sent is a still page, which sends one about every 160 ms, not a stall). `--prof` CPU-profiles the render worker and lists the busy runs; a site built with `mksite.sh` adds the worker's own events and its event-loop lateness (`lagMax`). Run main and a branch in turn (`SITE=`), as the GPU is shared.
